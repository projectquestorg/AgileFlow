# Product

## Problem

Small teams need one shared place to organize their work.

## Users

Team owners who set up a workspace, and the members they bring in.

## Outcomes

Teams can create a workspace, bring the right people in, and manage who has access.

## Principles

- Access changes take effect immediately.
- A workspace always has at least one owner.

## Non-goals

- Enterprise identity (SSO, SCIM).

## Definition of Done

A story is done when:
- its acceptance criteria are satisfied;
- `npm test` passes;
- the change has been reviewed and merged through a pull request.
