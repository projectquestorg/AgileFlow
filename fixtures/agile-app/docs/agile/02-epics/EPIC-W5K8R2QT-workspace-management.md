# Workspace management

## Problem

Owners cannot adjust a workspace after creating it, and members cannot leave one.

## Outcome

Owners and members can manage their workspace membership and settings themselves.

## Scope

- renaming a workspace
- leaving a workspace

## Non-goals

- transferring ownership (later)
- deleting workspaces

## Success

Nobody needs support to rename or leave a workspace.

## Constraints

A workspace always keeps at least one owner.
